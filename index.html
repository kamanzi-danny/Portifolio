*784# USSD Service

Water Access Rwanda's USSD self-service code. Customers dial *784# from any phone, with no internet needed, to:

  • Check their registered meter and account info
  • Buy water tokens via Mobile Money
  • Retrieve their latest paid token if it hasn't arrived by SMS yet
  • Check their water balance and subscription status
